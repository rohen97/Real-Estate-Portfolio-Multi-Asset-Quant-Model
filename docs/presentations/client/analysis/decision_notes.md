All values come from the final public snapshot or the same canonical action samples. All 6 income-floor plans pass 32 independent constraint checks with zero violations. The 70% point exactly reproduces the saved expected NPV and joint CVaR.

Use capital release versus minimum retained NOI as the main decision plot. This makes the policy trade-off tangible without overstating alpha. Capital release is the sale receipt, not economic profit; show the incremental NPV separately. Hold is the zero incremental reference, not risk-free investing.

For the action plot, x = 95% downside CVaR and y = expected incremental NPV, both S$ million. Label all five actions directly. Missing underwriting prevents sign-off.

For development, compare technical capacity with the detailed cash-flow hurdle: the illustrative site can fit 10,774 sqm, yet detailed monthly redevelopment incremental NPV is -S$37.02m and LBC is unassessed. Do not place this number beside the annual Redevelop screen (-S$11.37m) as if the assumptions were identical.

The twin experiment is separate synthetic data with different baseline NOI and constraints. Its stress environments demonstrate workflow responsiveness, not forecast accuracy or alpha.

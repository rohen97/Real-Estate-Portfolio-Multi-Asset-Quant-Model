# Singapore acquisition and disposal selection methodology

## Principle

Zoning is not a direct buy or sell instruction. It changes the value and risk of each available action. The model calculates an adjusted selection NPV for Hold, Retrofit, Repurpose, Redevelop and Sell, then reports a separate acquisition and portfolio signal.

## Inputs

- MP2025 statutory and economic capacity.
- Unused GFA and residual option value.
- En-bloc screening probability.
- Surrounding MP2025 polygons within 500 metres.
- View-block probability from nearby high-density zoning.
- Competing-supply pressure.
- Discounted Greater Southern Waterfront, Jurong Lake District and Paya Lebar Air Base exposure.
- Tenure and lease-decay risk.
- Public market momentum.
- Action-specific expected NPV and probability of loss.

## Action-specific treatment

Hold retains all future zoning benefits and all surrounding/lease risks. Retrofit retains most benefits and risks. Repurpose and Redevelop capture development value but reduce exposure to the existing asset's obsolescence. Sell receives only the market value already capitalised into the sale and avoids future operating/view/supply/lease penalties.

## Returns

Expected total return equals operating yield, market return and annualised incremental action value. Expected alpha equals expected total return less the required return.

## Governance

Catalogue-only assets receive Data Required / Monitor. Buy/Invest, Retain/Monitor and Sell/Avoid signals are enabled only after verified underwriting inputs replace proxies. Every signal includes the complete value and penalty decomposition and is an economic screening output, not a transaction instruction.
